Looking After Your Mouthguard:

  • DO rinse with water after use.
  • DO brush with toothbrush & toothpaste if necessary.
  • DO store the product in its protective case to protect it from being squashed or damaged.
  • DO NOT immerse/boil the product in hot water - It does not need to be moulded.
  • DO NOT soak or rinse it in any detergents.
  • DO NOT put it in the washing machine / tumble dryer / microwave.

Note: The mouthguard will retain its shape unless squashed or subjected to harsh chemicals or heat. For growing individuals, the mouthguard will eventually cease to fit comfortably, and at such point will require a new mouthguard.
